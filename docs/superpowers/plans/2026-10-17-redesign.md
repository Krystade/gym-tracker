# Redesign: Grouped, with a quiet Crew

The app feels rough: everything is a bordered box, amber tints go muddy, there is no type scale, every button looks the same, and emoji stand in for icons. The visual audit (`.superpowers/redesign/audit.md`) measured each of these. This round rebuilds the look on one token set and renames Orbit to Crew. Behaviour stays the same.

## Owner decisions (2026-10-17)

- **Direction A, "Grouped", for the whole app.**
  - Soft and iOS-native: a near-black page, inset grouped cells, hairline separators.
  - Containers have no borders. Only text fields keep a 3:1 boundary (WCAG 1.4.11).
  - Filled tonal buttons in three tiers: primary, secondary (tonal) and quiet (plain text).
  - One radius scale, an 8-pt spacing grid, and one type scale with a 34px large title.
  - A stepper is one rounded field, with − and + inside it.
  - The amber accent `#fbba4a` stays.
- **From B, "Scoreboard", only two things:** big tabular numerals for weight and reps, and the segmented "3/14 sets" progress bar.
- **Friends and Crew lean towards C, "Quiet".** That means flat lists, round avatar chips, pill buttons, lighter weights and a calm page. They use A's tokens and the amber accent, not C's teal.
- **Orbit becomes Crew everywhere.** That covers copy, files, identifiers, CSS classes and tokens, aria labels, comments and the plan doc. Nothing names the app that inspired it.
- **Crew colours are colour-blind safe.** The current set's closest pair is ΔE×100 4.5 under simulation. The new shared set's worst case is 17.9.
- **Emoji used as UI icons become inline SVG.** That means 🔔 🔕 ⋯ ✕ in the thread header and 💬 on the message button. The tab bar gets small SVG icons above its labels. Emoji that are content (pings, statuses, presets) stay emoji.
- **Subtle sheet and press motion,** off under `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- **The app icon does not change.**

## Base elements

- **Buttons:**
  - Secondary (the default `button`) is a tonal fill with no border.
  - `.primary` is an accent fill with accent-ink text. There is one per view where possible.
  - `.link`, `.quiet` and the plain text buttons (Skip, Swap, Edit, Details, Program) have no chrome and use accent text.
  - `.danger` uses danger text on the tonal fill.
  - All buttons keep 44px minimum taps.
  - Disabled primary stays opaque, with a 3:1 label.
- **Fields:** a 12px radius, an `--line-strong` 1px boundary, and a `--bg` or `--fill` interior. Focus shows a 2px accent outline.
- **Stepper:** one 52px rounded field (a `44px 1fr 44px` grid), with SVG − and + inside it and the value as 28px tabular numerals.
- **Chips:** a borderless tonal pill. Pressed is an accent fill with accent-ink text. Grouped choice rows (Energy, RIR, Program day) render as one segmented control.
- **Cells and cards:** a surface fill, a 12px radius, no border, and hairline separators inside lists.
- **Sheets:** a surface-2 fill, a 14px top radius, a grabber, and a soft shadow.
- **Tab bar:**
  - a surface-2 fill with a hairline on top
  - a 26px SVG icon above an 11px label
  - the active tab gets a filled icon and accent text
  - the Friends unread dot sits on its icon
- **Icons:** original 24px line icons at 1.75 stroke, in one `src/ui/icons.tsx`. They are `aria-hidden`, and the button keeps its label.

## Motion (stage 4)

- Sheets (thread, walkthrough, picker menus) slide up over 240ms with ease-out, and their backdrop fades in.
- A press scales to 0.97 over 60ms.
- Folds and disclosure chevrons rotate over 150ms.
- Under `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`, all of these are off. The existing reduced-motion rules for the walkthrough, spotlight and buttons stay.

## Tests that change on purpose

Each of these follows a deliberate design change. No other geometry check is loosened.

- **`design.spec`, "un-pressed chip border vs card 3:1".** Chips are borderless now. The check becomes two checks:
  - a pressed chip's fill against an un-pressed one is 3:1 or more, so the state shows (WCAG 1.4.11)
  - the un-pressed label on its fill is 4.5:1 or more
- **`spacing.spec`, prev/next arrows against the title.** The title moves under the arrows (A's large title). The check becomes:
  - the title starts below the arrows
  - the arrows don't overlap each other or Program
- **`spacing.spec`, block gap.** "12px to Add exercise" becomes 16px, the new block gap.
- **`time.spec`, "one line" heading.** "Shorter than 40px" assumed a 22px title. It becomes "shorter than one 41px line plus a half line", which still fails on a wrap.
- **`history.spec`, the pain mark colour.** It becomes the new `--danger`.
- **`devices.spec`.** The `.orbit-invite` selector becomes `.crew-invite`.
- **`polish.spec`, a folded plan card ends at its padding.** The padding is 16 now, so the allowance is 18 rather than 14.
- **`messages.spec` runs under reduced motion.** Its checks read where the thread sheet sits, not where it is mid-slide, as `walkthrough.spec` already does for the spotlight. A new `design.spec` check covers the motion itself: the sheet and backdrop animate with no preference and don't under reduce.
- **Instrument fixes.** The larger type and padding moved things the old checks happened to rely on. Each still fails on the bug it guards:
  - **`exercise.spec`.** The chart taps are by coordinates, which don't scroll. The chart is centred first, because its foot now fell under the tab bar and the tap went to History.
  - **`loop.spec`.** At the page's foot, Add set sat partly above the screen. Playwright's own scroll before the click then moved the page by 12px. The page is now nudged so Add set is wholly on screen. A mutation check (the floor turned off) still fails it, by 308px.
