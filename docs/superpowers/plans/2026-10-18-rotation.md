# Round 13: variation rotation

A lift that has fully plateaued is offered a swap to the next variation of the same movement pattern, aimed at the same
muscles. The idea: split training into six movement patterns with a few variations each, keep a variation while it still
progresses, and once it has plateaued for a couple of weeks rotate to the next one in the same pattern; strength carries
over, so you come back to the first one stronger. Nothing here changes by itself.

## Owner decisions (2026-10-18)

1. **Stall** = no new best e1RM and no rep or weight step-up for 3 sessions spanning at least 2 weeks on that lift. Working
   sets only: warm-ups, partials and test sets are left out as double progression already does (`isProgressionSet`).
2. **A suggestion, never automatic.** On the stalled lift's card on Today ("Bench Press stalled 3 weeks — switch to Incline
   Bench Press?") and on the lift's own screen. One tap replaces that lift's slots in the program, for good (the program is
   marked `edited`, as a hand edit is). Dismiss hides it until something changes.
3. **Grouping.** Six movement patterns as families over the catalog. The next variation is always in the same pattern, and
   among those the same bias comes first (the main muscle, the one a lift trains at 1 in `muscles.ts`, plus any other at ¾
   or more: a chin-up is lats and biceps, a pull-up lats), then the closest muscle profile. Lifts outside the six patterns (arms, calves, isolation, core, cardio, holds) never rotate. Only lifts the
   current gym can do are offered. Variations cycle: the least recently trained (or never trained) comes next, so after
   the last one you return to the first.
4. Double progression stays as it is; no fixed weekly +lb mode.
5. **Carried-over strength.** Switching seeds the new lift's first target from the old one's strength: the e1RM ratio
   between the two from your own history when both were trained around the same time, else a catalog ratio for lifts
   whose loads compare (barbell and Smith versions), else nothing (the new lift's own history, or "pick a weight").
   Shown as an estimate, and on the light side.
6. Nothing new to fill in. Variations stay their own exercises.

## The patterns

`src/domain/catalog.ts` gets `PATTERNS` and `VARIATIONS` (pattern → catalog lifts, in the order a new variation is
offered). Main muscle in brackets, from `muscles.ts`.

| Pattern | Variations |
|---|---|
| Horizontal press | Bench Press, Machine Chest Press, Incline Bench Press, Flat DB Press, Incline DB Press, Smith Flat Press, Smith Incline Press (chest); Close-Grip Bench Press (triceps) |
| Horizontal pull | Chest-Supported Row, Seated Cable Row, T-Bar Row, Kneeling DB Row (mid-back); Machine Row, Landmine Row (lats) |
| Vertical press | Overhead Press, Machine Shoulder Press, DB Shoulder Press, Arnold Press, Landmine Press (front delts) |
| Vertical pull | Lat Pulldown, Pull-up (lats); Close-Grip Lat Pulldown, Chin-up (lats, biceps ¾) |
| Squat | Barbell Squat, Leg Press, Smith Squat, Pendulum Squat, Front Squat, Bulgarian Split Squat, Landmine Squat (quads) |
| Hinge | Deadlift, Romanian Deadlift, DB Romanian Deadlift (hamstrings) |

Left out on purpose: push-ups and dips (bodyweight, and dips are neither press), straight-arm pulldown and lat pull-in
(isolation), hip thrust, glute press and back extension (glute or erector work, not the deadlift family).

A lift logged under its own name (not in the catalog) gets a pattern from keywords (`squat`, `leg press`, `hack` → squat;
`deadlift`, `RDL`, `good morning` → hinge; `row` → horizontal pull; `pulldown`, `pull-up`, `chin-up` → vertical pull;
`overhead`/`shoulder`/`military` press → vertical press; `bench`, `chest press`, `incline … press` → horizontal press),
unless it reads as isolation (the `defaultSettings` isolation words, plus straight-arm, pullover, upright row). Catalog lifts
not in the table get none.

## Domain: `src/domain/rotation.ts` (tests first: `rotation.test.ts`)

- `patternOf(name)`: the table, then the keywords for non-catalog names; null for holds and cardio.
- `stall(entries, lift, today)`: sessions of the lift up to today with progression sets, oldest first. A session
  *progresses* when, against the up-to-3 sessions before it within 6 weeks (so a peak from before a layoff doesn't count,
  and rebuilding after one is progress):
  - its best e1RM (`stats.e1rm`) beats theirs, or
  - its top weight is heavier than theirs (a weight step-up), or
  - at its top weight, one set has more reps than any of theirs at that weight, or its sets total more reps than each of
    theirs at that weight over the same number of sets (9/8/8 after 8/8/8 is progress; 9/8/7 after 8/8/8 is not).
  A lighter session with more reps is not a step-up unless its e1RM is a best; a weight zig-zag is not progress.

  A session with nothing before it in the window is a fresh start, so it progresses. Stalled when the sessions after the last one that progressed
  are 3 or more and span 14 days or more, and the latest is at most 6 weeks old (after a longer break the plateau is
  stale). Returns the last progress date, the session count and the weeks since it.
- `nextVariation(entries, lift, today, ctx)`: `ctx` has `available` (what this gym can do), `include` (lifts added to the
  gym) and `program`. The pool is the pattern's catalog lifts, your logged lifts with that pattern and the gym's added ones,
  minus the lift itself, lifts already in the program (no slot twice), lifts the gym can't do, and lifts with pain logged in
  the last 60 days. The pool's same-bias lifts are used when there are any, else the rest by muscle similarity. From
  those, the rotation is 3 variations: the lift plus the two others you trained most recently, topped up with ones you
  haven't trained (closest muscle profile, then table order). The next is the one of the two trained least recently, never
  trained first. That walks A → B → C → A.
- `rotation(entries, lift, today, ctx)`: both of the above, or null.
- `carryOver(entries, from, to, today, st)`: the new lift's estimated e1RM, from `from`'s current e1RM (best of its last 3
  sessions) times a ratio, times 0.9:
  - the ratio from your history: `to`'s e1RM over its last 3 sessions against `from`'s best within 6 weeks of `to`'s last
    session;
  - else the catalog ratio, only between lifts whose loads compare (Bench 1, Smith Flat 1, Close-Grip Bench 0.9, Incline
    Bench 0.8, Smith Incline 0.8; Overhead Press alone; Lat Pulldown 1, Close-Grip Lat Pulldown 1; Barbell Squat 1, Smith
    Squat 1, Front Squat 0.8; Deadlift 1, Romanian Deadlift 0.75). Dumbbells, machines and bodyweight lifts get none: how they're loaded
    varies too much;
  - none when `to` was trained in the last 6 weeks (its own progression is current) or there's no ratio.

  The weight is the one that estimate gives at the top of the range, rounded *down* to the lift's increment, for
  `repMin` reps: the same way a test set sets the working weight, then lower.
- `rotateProgram(program, from, to, range)`: every slot of `from` becomes `to` with `to`'s rep range (via
  `renameInProgram`, which already merges a slot that would repeat), `edited: true`.

## Progression: the seed

`ExerciseSettings` gets an optional `seed: { weight, reps, from, date }`. `nextTarget` uses it while the lift has no session
on or after `seed.date` (kind `carry`, "Estimate"); the first session after the switch takes over as usual. Settings
already sync as whole objects, so the seed rides along. `suggest` explains it: "Estimated from your Bench Press: a
conservative start. Adjust after the first set."

## UI

- `src/ui/RotationSuggestion.tsx`: the suggestion block in the `target-suggest` style. Title line "Bench Press stalled
  3 weeks — switch to Incline Bench Press?", one muted line why ("No new best in 3 sessions since 21 Sep · same pattern,
  same main muscle (chest)"), one line for the start ("Start ≈ 120 lb × 8 — an estimate from your Bench Press" / "Picks up
  from your last session of it" / "New to you: start light"). Buttons: **Switch to …** (when the lift is in the program)
  and **Dismiss**. Dismiss is kept per profile on this phone (`prefs`), keyed by the lift, holding the next variation and the
  last progress date: a new plateau, or a different next variation (another gym), shows it again.
- Today: on the lift's card, under its head, when the day is today, the lift is a slot in the program, and it has no
  working set logged today (once you've started it, the swap would only confuse today's plan). Switching saves the program,
  the seed, and today's stored plan with the lift renamed (if it mentions it); the card becomes the new lift in the same
  place with its estimated target, and a "Switched from … Undo" note sits under its head for 8 seconds. Undo puts back the program,
  the plan, the new lift's settings and the card.
- The lift's screen: under "Next time", whether or not it's in the program; without a slot, it's advice with Dismiss only.
  Switched from there, a note with Undo stays while the screen is open.

## Tests

- Unit (`rotation.test.ts`, synthetic logs): every table lift is in the catalog with gear needs, its main muscle as
  listed; keywords; stall (3 flat sessions over 14 days stalls; 2 sessions, or 13 days, doesn't; any of the three kinds of
  progress resets it; warm-ups, partials and tests are ignored; an old peak outside 13 weeks doesn't count; a stale plateau
  doesn't show; holds and isolation never stall); next variation (same pattern only, same main muscle first, falls back by
  similarity, gym availability, program lifts and recent pain excluded, cycles A → B → C → A, least recently trained
  first); carry-over (history ratio, catalog ratio, none for dumbbells/machines, none when recently trained, rounded
  down); `rotateProgram` (all days, range, `edited`, merge); `nextTarget` with a seed (used until a session on/after its
  date).
- e2e at 375×812 with the notch (`rotation.spec.ts`): an imported synthetic plateau on Bench Press, a one-day program;
  the card shows the suggestion, fits the width with 44 px buttons; a screenshot is read. Switch: the card, plan row and
  Program show Incline Bench Press with an estimated target; it survives a reload. Undo puts everything back. Dismiss
  survives a reload. The lift's screen shows it too.
- Mutation checks on the stall rules, the bias preference, the cycle order, availability, and the 0.9 discount.
