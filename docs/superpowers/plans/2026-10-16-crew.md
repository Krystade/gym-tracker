# Crew: every friend on one screen

A group page where each person is a glowing line across the days, so you see everyone's training and status at once.

## Owner decisions (2026-10-16)

- A group page under Friends shows **every friend on one screen**, plus you first.
- Each person is a **line across the days, coloured by what they trained**: legs, push, pull, arms and core, cardio. Training days glow; rest days are a thin dim line; today is marked.
- **Status bubble**: an optional short phrase each person sets for themselves (24 characters at most). Empty shows nothing. The app never asks for it.
- **Label under each line**: this week's numbers, "3 sessions · 41 sets".
- **Cycle data never appears here.** Cycle tracking is private (round 6 F).

## Data

- Everything comes from what friends can already read: `users/{uid}/months/*` and `users/{uid}/meta/*`. No new collections, no rules change except the status phrase (below).
- Only the months the visible window touches are read: the current and previous month doc per friend, not the whole history `readFriend` loads. A new `readFriendWindow(c, uid, from, to)` reads `meta/index`, then just those months. Cached per friend for the session; pulled again on a pull-down or when the page is opened after 5 minutes.
- **Status phrase**: kept in the profile meta items as `status` (string, trimmed, ≤ 24 chars, emoji allowed), so it syncs and friends read it with no new path. Rules: the existing meta write rule covers it; a rules test checks a friend can read it and can't write it.

## Session type per day (`domain/crew.ts`, pure)

- For each day, add up the muscle vectors (`muscleVector`) of that day's working sets, weighted by set count.
- Group into five kinds:
  - **Legs**: Quads, Hamstrings, Glutes, Calves, Adductors, Abductors.
  - **Push**: Chest, Triceps, Front Delts, Side Delts.
  - **Pull**: Lats, Mid-Back, Rear Delts, Biceps, Traps.
  - **Arms and core**: Abs, Forearms, Erectors.
  - **Cardio**: any cardio entry that day, by minutes.
- The day takes the kind with the largest share; a day where two kinds are within 20% of each other shows both, as a split segment (top half, bottom half).
- No working sets and no cardio: a rest day.
- `weekNumbers(entries, week)`: sessions (days with a working set or cardio) and working sets in the week.
- Unit tests: each kind wins on a clear day; a push/pull day within 20% gives both; a warm-up-only day is rest; cardio alone is cardio; unmapped lifts don't count toward a kind but still make it a training day (shown neutral).

## Screen (375 × 812, notch insets)

- Reached from More → Friends; it's the top of the Friends screen, above the list and requests.
- **Header**: a day strip (dates, weekday initials), 14 days visible on a phone, today ringed. Drag sideways to scroll every line together, back up to 8 weeks; "Today" chip jumps back.
- **Rows**, you first, then friends by most recent training:
  - Name, then the status bubble (tinted by their most recent kind's colour).
  - The line: one segment per day, kind colour with a soft glow on training days, a 2 px dim line on rest days, a dot at today.
  - Below: "3 sessions · 41 sets", this week.
- Tapping a row opens that friend's view (or your History for your own row). Tapping your own bubble edits the phrase inline: a text field, Save, Clear.
- A colour key (five dots with labels) once, under the header, folded after the first visit.
- **Empty states**: no friends yet shows your own row plus "Invite a friend" (the existing invite link); a friend with nothing in the window shows a dim line and "No sessions in 2 weeks".
- **Invite** button floats above the tab bar only when you have fewer than 3 friends; otherwise it lives in the list below.
- Colours come from tokens on `:root` with dark-theme values, chosen for contrast on `--bg`, and each segment also carries its kind in an accessible table (the chart-table pattern `LineChart` uses), so colour isn't the only signal.
- Performance: SVG per row, no canvas; at most ~20 friends × 56 days.

## Parts

1. `domain/crew.ts` + tests.
2. `readFriendWindow` + status phrase in meta (+ rules test).
3. The Crew screen section, own row first, with the status editor.
4. Drag-to-scroll window and the "Today" chip.
5. e2e at 375 × 812 with two emulator users: B logs a legs day and a push day, sets a status; A's Crew shows B's line with those kinds, the bubble, "2 sessions · N sets"; no horizontal page scroll; a screenshot looked at by eye. A third user who isn't a friend never appears.
