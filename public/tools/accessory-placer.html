Accessory Placer

placed for every held item
Nudge one pixel:
Send my marks to Claude

Marks save in this browser. The ones already in the app are where you start; what you change here wins on this phone. Copy them (all of them, the app’s included) and paste them into the chat so they can go into the app. To restore marks, paste them here and press Load.

Put the held item where you want it on a buddy’s first frame. Drag from anywhere on the stage: the item moves as far as your finger does, so it stays in sight. With two fingers, pinch to size it and twist to tilt it, all the way round. Stretch it with Width and Height, flip it with Mirror, nudge it, or type a number. Every frame you haven’t set shows a guess, learned from what you have set: this buddy’s other frames or poses first, then the buddies you’ve set that are shaped most like it, then its pack. Press Accept when a guess is right, or Accept all for this buddy; drag or nudge it when it isn’t. A grey Head anchor means nothing to learn from yet: the item where the app puts it unplaced. A green dot marks frames you’ve set, an amber ring guesses. Your work saves as you go. This is exactly what the app draws (the held slot: auras, plinths and backdrops go round the whole buddy, with nothing to place): on your buddies on this phone straight away, guesses included, and on everyone’s once you send your marks.
