Accessory Placer

Move one pixel:
Send my marks to Claude

Marks save in this browser. Copy them and paste them into the chat so they can go into the app. To restore marks, paste them here and press Load.

Pick a body slot, then drag its item into place in the first frame (the beanie onto the head, the shorts onto the hips, the dumbbell into the front hand) and set its size and tilt with the sliders or by typing a number. The tool then guesses the other frames by following that spot through the animation. Step through them: press Looks right when a guess is fine, or drag it to fix it. Your fixes become the starting point for the frames after them. A green dot marks frames you've set, an amber ring marks guesses. Your work saves as you go.
